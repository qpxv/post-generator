# learned prompt changelog

- 2026-10-05: edit rules relearned from 59 reviewed drafts
  - trim elaboration lines that repeat a point already made: a third item in a list, a 'not x, not y' negation line, or a line that spells out what the previous line implied. keep the strongest one or two beats and move on (11 pairs. pair 6: 'wanted to quit the exam sign out drop the whole thing' became 'wanted to quit the exam'. pair 24: cut 'no pressure no guilt no negotiation'. pair 25: cut 'not the testimonials')
  - don't end on a summary, moral, or slogan that restates the hook or the point already made. stop on the last concrete image or the line that makes the point, and cut the 1 to 3 lines after it. this includes tags like 'that is all' (8 pairs. pair 13: cut 'the details are not decoration / they are the whole argument'. pair 7: cut 'the work you put into your offer deserves a website that can hold the weight of it'. pair 11: cut 'that is all')
  - if you add a closing line, make it a short casual aside or blunt tag that adds something new, such as a joke, a concrete detail, or a quick verdict. don't use a polished takeaway (5 pairs. pair 31: added 'couldnt be me'. pair 38: added 'its binary'. pair 30: added 'even though it technically "looks good"'. pair 15: added 'and thats an entirely different skillset than moving around rectangles')
  - put the 'not x' or contrast half of a sentence on its own line instead of joining it to the main clause. a lone line for the punch lands harder (4 pairs. pair 17: 'the website was INVISIBLE not broken' became 'the website was INVISIBLE' / 'not broken'. pair 12: 'cognitive work FOR your visitors not assigning it to them' split into two lines. pair 16: 'a snapshot of who they were not who they are' split into two lines)
  - don't attach specific years to dated-design jabs ('2019 squarespace template', 'stock photo from 2017', 'made in 2014'). name the platform, or use a plain concrete flaw such as slow load or the wrong template (3 pairs. pair 29: 'a 2019 squarespace template' became 'a squarespace template'. pair 27: 'hero image that looked like a stock photo from 2017' became 'hero image that loaded slow'. pair 38: 'looks like it was made in 2014' became 'looks like the wrong template got used')
- 2026-10-08: edit rules relearned from 65 reviewed drafts
  - end the post on the last concrete beat and cut the final one or two lines when they restate the lesson, give a moral, or add a sign-off like 'that is all'. (9 pairs (1, 5, 6, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 37). e.g. draft ended 'the details are not decoration / they are the whole argument' and published ended on 'subtly tells people the opposite'.)
  - when a draft stacks three or more parallel 'not x' or 'no x' lines, keep only the one or two that carry the point and cut the rest. (5 pairs (12, 16, 28, 29, 39). e.g. 'not the words / not the offers / not the testimonials / the FEELING' became 'not the words / not the offers / the FEELING'.)
  - state general truths and claims about how things work in the present tense, not the past. (4 pairs (1, 2, 3, 4). e.g. 'your visitors only ever saw one / so yours had to be the one that won' became 'only ever see one / so yours has to be the one that won'.)
  - when a line ends with a 'not x' contrast or joins two clauses with no break, put the contrast or second clause on its own line. (4 pairs (16, 19, 20, 21). e.g. 'the website was INVISIBLE not broken' became 'the website was INVISIBLE' / 'not broken'.)
  - where the draft makes a point and moves on, add a short casual or deadpan aside of one line. it can be a joke, a blunt kicker, or a flat clarification. don't add a polished moral. (6 pairs (4, 19, 34, 35, 40, 42). e.g. added 'that's evolutionary', 'its binary', 'couldnt be me', and 'and thats an entirely different skillset than moving around rectangles'.)
  - drop years and dated comparisons ('from 2017', 'made in 2014', '2019 squarespace template') and use a plain generic detail instead. (3 pairs (31, 33, 42). e.g. 'a 2019 squarespace template' became 'a squarespace template'; 'looked like it was made in 2014' became 'looks like the wrong template got used'.)
  - don't write long posts where a quirky personal experiment is explained in step-by-step mechanism (kilos of blanket weight, a quoted axiom, a spelled-out inference chain) before a stretched 'visitors do the exact same thing' link to websites. both deleted drafts did this and then needed a reply with a dm pitch. (2 deleted drafts: the weighted-blanket / eleven-field contact form post and the lowercase follow-ups / 'axiom 7 contextual inference' post. published posts with the same reply format used shorter, simpler analogies.)
